常见问题

需要注册账号吗？
不需要。Cody Robot 没有账号，数据留在你的 iPhone 上。

为什么要填 API Key？
Cody Robot 用你自己选择的 AI 服务商（如 DeepSeek、通义千问、Kimi）来回答。模型费用由服务商直接向你收取，我们不经手。设置 → 模型里粘贴 Key 即可，Key 存在 iOS 钥匙串里。

还没有 Key，能先看看效果吗？
可以。在模型列表里选「演示模式」，不联网、不需要 Key，会完整回放一次写代码并运行的过程。

怎么取消订阅？
iPhone「设置 → Apple 账户 → 订阅」里找到 Cody Robot 取消；App 内「设置 → 通用 → 订阅」也有入口。取消后当期结束前仍可使用。

某条回答不当怎么办？
点回答下方的旗帜图标「举报」，选原因后会打开一封发给我们的邮件。

FAQ

Do I need an account?
No. Cody Robot has no accounts; your data stays on your iPhone.

Why do I need an API key?
Cody Robot uses the AI provider you choose. The provider bills you directly; we never handle it. Paste your key in Settings → Models; it is stored in the iOS Keychain.

Can I try it without a key?
Yes. Pick "Demo mode" in the model list. It works offline with no key and replays a complete write-and-run-code session.

How do I cancel my subscription?
iPhone Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions, or in the app under Settings → General → Subscription. You keep access until the end of the current period.

An answer was inappropriate.
Tap the flag icon under the answer to report it; it opens an email to us.
